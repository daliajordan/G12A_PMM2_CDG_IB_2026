## Discussion

### Identification of PMM2 and the disease-associated variant

The variant is a substitution of nucleotides in PMM2 C to T in the position 710 (c.710C>T) causing a missense variation which changes codon ACG to ATG resulting in the gene codifying for the Methionine amino acid  instead of Threonine. Although they are both uncharged, they have different properties. Threonine is polar and has an hydroxyl group which allows it to form hydrogens bonds. But, it is replaced by Methionine which doesn’t have an hydroxyl group disrupting protein interactions. From Briso-Montiano et al.(2022), the side chain of THr237 forms a hydrogen bond with Thr16, coordinating the Mg-1. The loss of this bond has indeed an effect on the stability of the protein and the enzymatic activity leading to malfunction. However, the variant kept a similar structure to the wild-type protein , so a change in the sequence doesn’t necessarily alter the structure of the protein.

We identified that 237 is located in alpha-9 helix, not in a Mg2+ binding site, but 11 residues after the Thr226. Mg2+ helps to conduct the enzymatic reaction of PMM2. The substitution is not likely going to affect the union of the Magnesium. However, since we don't have any interactions documented  we can't  demonstrate that it may be an impact through the loss of the bond.

PMM2 converts mannose-1-phosphate from mannose-6-phosphate, important for GDP-mannose required for glycosylation. An altered PMM2 enzymatic activity could affect the reaction contributing to the congenital disorder (PMM2-CDG).


### Structural comparison of wild-type and T237M PMM2

From the superposition of 7O1B (wild-type) and 7O58 (mutant) we obtained a low RMSD (0.351 Å). The backbone is the main chain of the protein, we got that the difference between them is also low (0.262 Å). This means that the change of an amino acid didn’t have a noticeable impact on the structure of the protein, they are very similar with little differences.However, RMSD only tells us the positions but it doesn’t ientify modifications between the interactions of the chains. So, such subtle changes doesn't mean that they don't provoke consequences related to the functionality and enzymatic activity.

### Cross-species comparison of gene and protein sequences

The comparisons with different species, Leishmania mexicana and Vicugna pacos showed us the conservation of the sequences and its importance. Starting with V.pacos, it was the most similar to the human.(91% identical sequence of nucleotides,but only 62 % of the sequence aligned). At the protein level (91% identity with 87% of the sequence aligned) shows a great resemblance in the sequence. But, the 87% of the alignment is due to the fact that is an isoform and the protein ends at the 213 residue lacking the resiudes from 214 to 246.
For Leishmania mexicana, it was possible only to align the 21 % of the CDS, with 70% identity.It tells that L.mexicana is more different, had changed throughout evolution. Regarding the protein we aligned 97 % of the sequence with 57% identity and 71% positives, which includes not only identical but different with similar chemical properties. So, it makes sense that protein positives have a higher percentage than the identity. 
Overall,we obtained a bigger similitude of the human nucleotide sequences with the Vicugna Pacos. We had a bigger sequence to study and almost all of them were identical. The differences in percentages of CDS and proteins mean that the nucleotide sequences have changed more than the proteins. Furthermore, there are codons in V.pacos different from homo sapiens but in result encode the same amino acids.

The three species in the position 11-17 of PMM2 had different nucleotides but in result they encoded for the same amino acids (FDVDGTL). This motif has another inside, the DxD, which includes Asp14, Asp12, which participate in the magnesium binding, are identical in the three species. This means that in this position that specific amino acid has an importance for the protein, structurally or functionally, that's why evolution conserved the same sequence of amino acids. The fact that the sequences are in three different species means that it’s important to have them in this order to maintain the function of the protein. A mutation could be perjudical.
The Thr16 was conserved in the motif, it suggests that the interaction with Thr237 could be relevant so it was preserved in the species.

 But again, conservation gives us evidence that the regions are significant  but we would need more direct experimentation to give a certain conclusion.

### Structural comparison between human and *L. mexicana*

The RMSD of the L.mexicana and the human is 1,66 Å, telling  us that the structure  is similar, despite having only 56,4 % of sequence identity. It suggests that having a quite large percent of different amino acids  doesn’t mean that the structure of the protein can change drastically. In our case, although having differences in the sequence, the structure of PMM2 L.Mexicana and human PMM2 have a similar fold only between the residues 65 and 84 has some differences.

The three-dimensional structure is important to deduce how the molecules interact with each other, but having similar structures,folds doesn’t imply that the activity of the protein is going to be the same. Local interactions and differences in the side chains of amino acids might affect the functionality of the protein.

The residue 237 is located inside the alpha-helix in both structures with a local backbone displacement of 1.465 Å, indicating a bit of difference of position between the two structures.It is higher than the comparison between the wild-type and human since the sequence in L.mexicana diverged more.
 The alpha-helix is a secondary form of the protein shaped in a spiral way, contributing to protein folding stabilised by interactions. However, having a positional variation in this section doesn’t demonstrate an alteration in its stability.

Regarding the comparison with the Vicugna pacos, which we saw earlier that it’s very similar to the human PMM2 sequence, the protein sequence which included our residue 237 wasn’t available. So, we couldn’t study if the residue is conserved nor compare it structurally.

### Limitations

We couldn’t make a proper analysis due to several limitations that we should consider. First of all, the RMSD and local backbones provide only information about the differences in the positions of the protein but not the function. We don’t have any analysis that determines the enzymatic activity or the stability of the hydrogen bond. . So we can’t determine that the differences in structure affect the PMM2 function.

Secondly,in the comparison of the species we hadn’t available all the regions of the sequences, so we depended on what we obtained. For instance, we only had avalaible 21% of the Leishmania Mexicana sequence aligned with the human leading to our conclusions being limited. Furthermore,
the Vicugna pacos is an isoform that lacks the region which includes our 237 residue, so we only could analyse the conservation with the other species.  

Also, there were gaps in the sequence alignment so that regions we should have analyse it and interpret carefully. 

 Lastlty, our analyses can’t fully confirm the consequences in functionality of the variant T237M which alters PMM2 function. Further experiments of the stability would be needed to clarify how the substitution affects the gene. Although the strucutural comparisons with the PMM2 revealed a similarity between the species we would need additional analysis of the enzymatic activity to determine if the function is conserved.
